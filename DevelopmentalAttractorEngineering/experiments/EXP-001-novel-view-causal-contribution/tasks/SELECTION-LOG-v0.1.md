# EXP-001 Task Selection Log v0.1

Selection performed before experimental administration.

## Neutral selection criteria

Selected tasks should collectively provide:

- a concrete recommendation;
- sufficient information for a useful answer without research;
- heterogeneous mundane domains;
- plausible room for materially different reasoning;
- low dependence on specialist factual knowledge;
- no DAE terminology or explicit instruction to find hidden stakeholders, second-order effects, incentives, bottlenecks, or other target structures;
- two open/underdetermined tasks;
- two robust controls where gratuitous complication should not improve the answer.

The candidate pool was authored in the schema-aware conversation under the limitation recorded in Amendment 001. No experimental condition outputs had yet been generated.

## Robust controls

## Predeclared cues for C ability probes

Cues are not answer keys and are never supplied to D, G, or S.

- **T02:** Automation may change the composition of the work left to human agents: straightforward cases disappear from their queue while difficult or emotionally demanding cases become a larger share of human work.
- **T03:** Average eating time may hide unequal effects: pupils who queue longer, need more time to eat, require assistance, or use lunch for recovery/social regulation could lose disproportionately from a shorter break.
- **T04:** Free parking may change behaviour rather than merely transfer cost: it can induce extra driving, alter parking turnover, and affect congestion or other transport modes.
- **T05:** Raising picking throughput may move rather than remove the operational bottleneck if packing or courier collection cannot absorb the additional flow.
- **T06:** Running systems in parallel buys reversibility and information: the extra cost may be an option premium that allows rollback if unknown migration failures appear.
- **T08:** Rewarding ticket counts may alter which tickets employees choose and when they close them, improving the metric without equivalent improvement in service quality.
- **T09:** The cheaper venue shifts some of the saved organizer cost onto attendees through travel time, transport cost, and accessibility burden, and that burden may not be evenly distributed.
- **T20:** The expected cash benefit of reducing inventory may be small relative to the asymmetric consequence of a rare component shortage when that single component can halt production.

## Why these twelve

The eight cue-bearing tasks span automation, school policy, transport policy, operations, software rollout, incentives, event planning, and inventory. The two open tasks test whether the schema can generate useful differentiation without a benchmark-authored candidate view. The robust controls test whether structured differentiation becomes gratuitous instability.

No task is selected because a schema output has already demonstrated success on it.
