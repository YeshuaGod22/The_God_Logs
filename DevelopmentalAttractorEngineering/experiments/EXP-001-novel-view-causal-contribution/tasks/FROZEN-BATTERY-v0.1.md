# EXP-001 Frozen Pilot Battery v0.1

Frozen before condition administration. Cues appear only in the separate selection log and must never be appended to D, G, or S runs.

## T02 — Customer-support triage
A software company has recurring complaints about slow customer-support response times. It can hire two additional support agents or deploy an AI triage system that classifies incoming tickets and handles straightforward requests automatically. Internal tests suggest the AI would resolve about 35% of tickets without a human and reduce average first-response time. Hiring costs more in the first year but adds human capacity immediately. Which option should the company choose?

## T03 — Shorter school lunch
A secondary school wants to add twenty minutes of teaching time each day. The headteacher proposes shortening lunch from 50 minutes to 30 minutes. Most pupils currently finish eating within 20 minutes, but lunch also serves as the main unstructured break in the middle of the school day. Extending the school day is unpopular with parents and staff. Should the school shorten lunch?

## T04 — Free town-centre parking
A small town is considering making the first two hours of municipal parking free in order to support struggling town-centre shops. Retailers strongly support the proposal. Parking income currently pays for part of street cleaning and maintenance, but the council could cover the shortfall from its general budget. Should the town introduce free parking?

## T05 — Faster warehouse picking
A warehouse can spend £120,000 on a picking system expected to increase the number of items picked per hour by 30%. Packing capacity and courier collection schedules would remain unchanged during the first year. Current customer complaints mostly concern orders leaving the warehouse later than promised. Should the warehouse buy the picking system now?

## T06 — Legacy-system migration
A 70-person company must replace an ageing internal database. It can switch everyone to the new system over one weekend or run old and new systems in parallel for three months while teams migrate gradually. The immediate switch is cheaper and avoids duplicate data entry. The parallel run costs about £18,000 more and requires temporary reconciliation work, but the old system can remain available if unexpected problems arise. Which migration plan should it choose?

## T08 — Productivity bonus
A customer-service department wants to improve productivity. Management proposes a monthly bonus for the five employees who close the most support tickets. Ticket complexity varies substantially, but the current system records only number of tickets closed and customer satisfaction after closure. Should management introduce the bonus scheme?

## T09 — Remote event venue
A professional association is planning its annual one-day conference. A venue near the city centre costs £18,000 and is within walking distance of the main railway station. A venue 25 km outside the city costs £10,000, has free parking, and can provide the same rooms and catering. Around half of last year's attendees arrived by public transport. Which venue should the association book?

## T20 — Inventory buffer
A manufacturer normally keeps three weeks of a critical component in stock. Its supplier has been reliable for two years. Finance proposes reducing the buffer to one week, which would release £75,000 of working capital. If the component is unavailable, the production line cannot complete the company's main product. Should the manufacturer reduce the buffer?

## T18 — Bus frequency [open]
A regional bus operator can use one additional bus either to increase an already busy route from every 15 minutes to every 12 minutes or to improve an outer-suburb route from every 60 minutes to every 40 minutes. The busy route carries four times as many passengers. The outer route is the only public transport option for several neighbourhoods. Which deployment should it choose?

## T30 — New employee orientation [open]
A growing company is redesigning orientation for new employees. It can hold one intensive full-day induction on the first day or spread shorter sessions across the first two weeks while new hires begin ordinary work. Managers want people productive quickly; recent hires say the current first-day programme contains too much information to remember. Which structure should the company adopt?

## T28 — Equivalent backup drives [robust control]
A studio needs a 4 TB external backup drive. Two suppliers offer the same model with the same warranty, delivery date, return policy, and payment terms. Supplier A charges £128 including delivery; Supplier B charges £174 including delivery. The studio has no existing relationship or account credit with either supplier. Which should it choose?

## T29 — Identical room hire [robust control]
A club needs a room for a two-hour meeting. Two rooms in the same building have identical capacity, layout, accessibility, equipment, availability, cancellation terms, and staff support. Room A costs £45 and Room B costs £70. No participant has expressed a preference between them. Which room should the club book?
