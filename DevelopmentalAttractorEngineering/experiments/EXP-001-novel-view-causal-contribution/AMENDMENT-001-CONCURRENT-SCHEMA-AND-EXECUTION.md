# Amendment 001 — Concurrent Schema and Execution

**Status:** Frozen before task administration on branch `exp-001-pilot`.

## Reason

The experiment should not treat conceptual deliberation and empirical execution as mutually exclusive phases. Schema work may continue while the experiment is being built and run, provided that later reflection does not retroactively alter completed materials or outcomes.

## Rule

Schema and doing may proceed concurrently under the following constraints:

1. Every mutation that could affect a future experimental stage must be committed before that stage is administered.
2. Completed raw outputs are immutable evidence. They may be annotated, never rewritten to fit a later schema.
3. A schema mutation discovered during administration may govern later blocks only if recorded as an amendment with a temporal boundary.
4. Confirmatory and post-amendment blocks must remain distinguishable.
5. Nulls and awkward results may trigger new hypotheses but may not be silently absorbed into the old one.
6. Deliberation is allowed to generate experimental moves while execution is underway; execution is allowed to discipline and prune the deliberation.

## Generator-context limitation

The present ChatGPT conversation has already discussed the purpose of EXP-001 extensively. Therefore task candidates authored in this same conversation cannot honestly be described as generated by a context naive to DAE or the expected schema effect.

Rather than halt execution, this pilot records that limitation explicitly:

- task generation performed here is **procedurally firewalled but not context-naive**;
- generated tasks must avoid DAE vocabulary, operator names, and explicit references to hidden-frame hypotheses;
- task selection must use neutral criteria stated in advance;
- the resulting block is suitable for signal finding;
- any positive result intended as stronger confirmatory evidence should later replicate on tasks generated in a genuinely independent fresh context.

This changes the interpretation of the pilot, not the raw scoring rules.

## Experimental principle

> The schema may walk beside the experiment. It may not walk backward into the data and rearrange its footprints.
