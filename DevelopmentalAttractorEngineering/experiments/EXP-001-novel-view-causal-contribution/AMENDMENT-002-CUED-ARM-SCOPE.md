# Amendment 002 — Scope of the Cued Ability Arm

**Frozen before administration.**

The preregistration describes four principal conditions per task, but the selected battery contains two open tasks and two robust controls for which no independently specified candidate cue is needed.

To avoid manufacturing arbitrary cues merely to fill a matrix, the pilot will administer:

- **D, G, S** on all 12 tasks;
- **C** only on the 8 latent-frame tasks with predeclared cues in `tasks/SELECTION-LOG-v0.1.md`.

Total principal runs before ablation: **44**.

This amendment narrows the C arm without changing P1, P2, P4, or P5. P3 (Ability ≠ Availability) is evaluated only on the eight cue-bearing tasks.

No condition outputs had been generated when this amendment was frozen.
